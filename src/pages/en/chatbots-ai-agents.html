Capabilities

What a well-configured AI agent does

The best of both worlds: the AI converses naturally and understands your customer, while button menus guide the steps that must be exact. It always knows when to hand off to a person.

Trained on your information

Upload your catalogs, policies, FAQs and manuals. The agent answers from them, in your brand's tone, and does not make up what it does not know.

Acts on intent

It detects whether the customer wants to buy, complain, ask or book, and follows the right path: it quotes, books, logs the complaint or routes the case.

Hands off to a person with context

When the case calls for it, it transfers the conversation to an agent with the full history and a summary. The customer does not have to repeat anything.

Visual no-code flows

Collections, appointments, lead capture, surveys. Built by dragging blocks; each customer reply triggers the next step.

All channels in one inbox

WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, Telegram and web chat, with the same agent and the same history per contact.

Connected to your systems

It checks balances, creates cases or logs orders in your core system, CRM or ERP through the REST API, webhooks, Zapier, Make or Google Sheets.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?
A classic chatbot follows a script: menus, buttons and fixed answers. An AI agent understands what the customer writes in their own words, answers with your company's information and decides the next step: resolve it, ask for a detail or hand off to a person. In practice we combine both: fixed flows for what must be exact (a payment, an appointment) and AI for conversation.
Do I need a technical team to use it?
No. We set up the flows, train the AI on your documents and train your team. Day-to-day changes are made in a visual editor, with no coding.
Can the AI make mistakes or make up answers?
It can, if left without limits. That is why we train it only on your information, give it clear instructions on what to answer and what not to, and set up handoff to a human agent for anything it should not resolve on its own. We also review conversations with your team during the first weeks to fine-tune.
Which AI models does it work with?
The platform works with ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), Gemini (Google) and other models. We choose the model for each case; in corporate plans the AI is included with a stated monthly allowance.
Can it also handle Instagram or my website?
Yes. The same agent handles WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, Telegram and your website chat, with a unified history per contact.
Which channels does the platform connect to?
The official WhatsApp Cloud API, Facebook Messenger (inbox and comments), Instagram (direct messages and comments), Telegram and your website's web chat. Everything arrives in a unified inbox with history per contact, team roles per user and conversations assigned per agent. SMS and bulk email are managed from their own platforms and integrate with yours through the API.
Does it integrate with my CRM, core system or app?
Yes. The platform has a documented REST API and inbound and outbound webhooks: your systems can create contacts, trigger messages and receive every event in real time (message received, conversation closed, data captured). It also connects with Zapier, Make, Google Sheets and hundreds of apps with no coding, and the AI can query your systems through MCP.
What can I automate?
Service and collections flows with a visual builder, bulk campaigns through the official WhatsApp API, automatic lead follow-up, bookings and appointments with a built-in scheduler, catalog and orders inside the chat, questionnaires and data capture, and automated replies to Facebook and Instagram comments.
Does it help with sales, not just service?
Yes. It includes a CRM-style pipeline to qualify leads, automatic assignment of each lead to a salesperson, a metrics dashboard and Facebook conversion tracking, so you know which campaign generated each sale.
What happens to my customers' data?
It is yours. The platform runs on Google Cloud, and across all our platforms we use top-tier servers with enterprise security tools. We work in line with Ecuador's Personal Data Protection Law and never use or share your contact lists.

Payment methods

Choose the one that suits you best. Services are activated once the payment has cleared.

Bank transfer or deposit

We accept transfers and deposits at Banco Pichincha and Banco del Pacífico. Transfers from other banks may take hours or days to clear; the service is activated once the funds are in our account.

Credit or debit card

Secure online payment through local or international payment gateways, depending on the service and where you are paying from. Before you pay, we let you know which gateway will be used. The gateway processes the payment with its own anti-fraud controls, and we do not store your full card details.

Cash money transfers

Send your payment from Ecuador or from abroad through Western Union or MoneyGram. Message us and we will send you the beneficiary details.

PayPal

Pay with your PayPal balance or the cards linked to your account, without sharing their details with us. A practical option if you are paying from outside Ecuador. For recurring services, you can set up an automatic payment agreement and cancel it whenever you want.

Every purchase goes through our fraud management process. Questions about accepted banks or cards? Message us at +593 98 777 7007 or write to info@masiva.ec.

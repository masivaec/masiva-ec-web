Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What services does MASIVA offer?
The official WhatsApp Cloud API, chatbots and AI agents, SMS to every carrier in Ecuador and to more than 200 countries with published rates per destination, bulk and transactional email, plus web design, hosting and domains. One provider, with human support in Ecuador.
How is your omnichannel platform different from your SMS and email services?
The omnichannel platform brings WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, Telegram and web chat into a single inbox, with history per contact and conversations assigned to agents. SMS and email are bulk channels with their own platforms and APIs. Everything connects to your systems, and the same MASIVA team supports you across all three.
Does it integrate with my CRM or my system?
Yes, through a documented REST API and inbound and outbound webhooks, and also with Zapier, Make and Google Sheets with no coding.
Can you send SMS outside Ecuador?
Yes, to more than 200 countries from the SMS platform, with the rate for each destination published on the SMS page.

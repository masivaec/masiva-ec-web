Summary of this policy

Who we are. MASIVA-EC S.A. is an Ecuadorian digital communications company serving businesses (text messaging, WhatsApp Business Platform API, email, virtual assistants and omnichannel platforms).

Two different roles. We are the controller of the data of our clients, prospects, suppliers, staff and visitors to our websites. However, when a company uses our platforms to message its own customers, that company decides on that data and we only process it on its behalf, as a processor.

If you received a message from a company through our platforms, section 9 explains whom to contact and how to stop receiving messages.

What we do not do. We do not sell or rent personal data, we do not use our clients' contact lists for our own purposes and we do not train artificial intelligence models with them.

Your rights. You can request access, rectification, updating, deletion, objection, portability or suspension, and withdraw your consent, by writing to datospersonales@masiva.ec. We respond within fifteen (15) days.

1. Purpose and commitment

This Policy explains what personal data MASIVA-EC S.A. (hereinafter, MASIVA) processes, for what purposes and on what legal bases, for how long, with whom it is shared, how it is protected and how data subjects can exercise their rights. Its purpose is that anyone whose information reaches MASIVA can understand clearly and completely how it is processed, in compliance with Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection and its implementing regulations.

MASIVA treats personal data protection as an essential part of its service. Its clients entrust it with information about their own users, and that trust can only be sustained with clear rules, real security measures and respect for each data subject's decisions.

3. Scope

3.1. To whom it applies

This Policy applies to all personal data that MASIVA processes, in any format, regarding the following persons:

  1. Legal representatives, notification contacts, technical contacts, operational contacts and other persons designated by MASIVA's clients.
  2. Prospects, meaning people who request information, respond to an ad or contact MASIVA through any of its channels without having contracted yet.
  3. Users authorized by clients to operate MASIVA's platforms.
  4. Recipients of the messages that clients send through MASIVA's platforms, for whom MASIVA acts as a processor under section 9.
  5. Suppliers, contractors and their representatives and contacts.
  6. Job applicants, employees and professionals who provide services to MASIVA.
  7. Visitors to MASIVA's websites and users of its digital channels.

3.2. Platforms and websites covered

The Policy applies to the masiva.ec and chatbot.ec websites, the app.chatbot.ec platform and any other platforms, subdomains and applications that MASIVA operates, under whatever brand name they are presented to the public. MASIVA and MENSSAJE are trademarks registered with Ecuador's National Intellectual Property Service (SENADI), lawfully used by MASIVA.

3.3. Mandatory nature

Compliance is mandatory for MASIVA's shareholders, directors, staff and collaborators involved in processing personal data, and for suppliers that process data on MASIVA's behalf, under the terms of their respective contracts.

4. Legal framework

MASIVA's processing of personal data is governed by the Constitution of the Republic of Ecuador, the Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (hereinafter, LOPDP), its General Regulations, the rules issued by the Superintendency of Personal Data Protection (hereinafter, SPDP), the Law on Electronic Commerce, Electronic Signatures and Data Messages, the telecommunications rules applicable to sending messages and other provisions in force. In services provided through third-party channels, MASIVA also observes those providers' policies, in particular those of Meta Platforms, Inc. for the WhatsApp Business Platform API and those of the telecommunications carriers for the SMS channel.

5. Definitions

The terms in this Policy have the meaning given to them by the LOPDP. For ease of reading:

  1. Personal data: information that identifies or makes identifiable a natural person, directly or indirectly.
  2. Sensitive data: data relating to ethnicity, gender identity, cultural identity, religion, ideology, political affiliation, criminal record, immigration status, sexual orientation, health, biometric data, genetic data and any data whose improper processing could give rise to discrimination.
  3. Data subject: the natural person to whom the data belongs.
  4. Processing: any operation on personal data, such as collection, recording, storage, consultation, use, disclosure, transfer or deletion.
  5. Controller: whoever decides on the purpose and means of processing.
  6. Processor: whoever processes personal data on behalf of and under the instructions of a controller.
  7. Sub-processor: a supplier hired by the processor to carry out part of the entrusted processing.
  8. Message recipient: a person to whom a MASIVA client sends messages through MASIVA's platforms.
  9. Suppression List: a technical record of numbers or addresses that must not receive messages, because they requested to opt out, filed complaints or are invalid destinations.
  10. Security breach: an incident affecting the confidentiality, integrity or availability of personal data.

6. Principles we apply

MASIVA processes personal data in accordance with the principles of the LOPDP, which in its operations translate as follows:

  1. Lawfulness and fairness: we only process data with a valid legal basis and never by deceptive means.
  2. Transparency: we explain in simple terms what we do with data, through this Policy and, where applicable, through notices in the forms and channels where we collect data.
  3. Purpose limitation: we use data only for the stated purposes and not for other incompatible ones.
  4. Relevance and data minimization: we request only the data needed for each purpose.
  5. Proportionality: we choose the least intrusive form of processing that achieves the purpose.
  6. Confidentiality: data is accessible only to those who need to know it.
  7. Quality and accuracy: we keep the data we control up to date; the data in our clients' contact lists is the responsibility of whoever provides it.
  8. Storage limitation: we keep data only for the periods in section 17.
  9. Security: we apply the technical and organizational measures in section 18.
  10. Accountability: we document and review the measures that allow us to demonstrate compliance.
  11. Interpretation in favor of the data subject: when in doubt about the scope of this Policy, we interpret it in the way most favorable to the data subject.

7. MASIVA's two roles

7.1. MASIVA as controller

MASIVA is the controller of the data it collects for its own management: its relationship with clients and prospects, hiring and paying suppliers, managing its staff, securing its platforms and serving people on its websites and channels. These processing activities are described in section 8.

7.2. MASIVA as processor

When a company hires MASIVA's services and, through its platforms, sends messages, handles conversations or manages contacts of its own customers, members or users, that company is the controller: it decides whom to write to, what to say, when, and on what legal basis. MASIVA acts as a processor and processes that data solely on the company's behalf, following its instructions and the contract between them. This role is described in section 9.

The distinction matters to data subjects, because it determines before whom they must exercise their rights and who is responsible for the decision to send them a message.

9. If you received a message from a company through our platforms

Many companies, including credit unions, financial institutions, retailers and organizations, use MASIVA's platforms to send text messages, WhatsApp messages or emails to their own customers, members or users. In those cases:

  1. Who decides about your data: the company that wrote to you. It obtained your number or email, chose to send you the message and must have a legal basis for doing so. MASIVA only provides the technology and does not use your data for its own purposes.
  2. How to stop receiving messages: use the opt-out mechanism indicated in the message itself or reply in the same conversation asking not to receive more messages. You can also ask the sending company directly.
  3. If you write to MASIVA: you can send your request to datospersonales@masiva.ec, stating the number or email that received the message and, if you know it, the sending company. MASIVA will forward your request to that company within five (5) business days, will work with it to handle the request and, to the extent technically possible, will add your number or address to the Suppression List of the corresponding account. The substantive response regarding your rights is the responsibility of the sending company.
  4. What MASIVA does not do: it does not sell or share your data with other clients, does not use it for its own advertising or to train artificial intelligence models, and does not identify other companies that may hold your number independently.

The rules of the processing arrangement are set out in the Data Processing Agreement that MASIVA signs with each client, and the clients' obligations regarding consent, content and opt-outs are set out in MASIVA's Acceptable Use Policy.

10. Sensitive data and children and adolescents

MASIVA does not request sensitive data for its own purposes, except for the staff health data described in section 8.5, which is processed with restricted access and only to the extent required by law.

MASIVA's services are aimed at businesses and not at children or adolescents. MASIVA does not intentionally collect data from minors. Under the LOPDP, adolescents aged fifteen (15) and older may give their own consent when the purpose is clearly explained to them; in all other cases, authorization from their legal representative is required. If MASIVA detects data from minors collected without meeting these requirements, it will delete it.

In its role as processor, MASIVA contractually prohibits its clients from uploading sensitive data or data of minors to the platforms without the enhanced legal basis required by law.

11. Recipients of the data

MASIVA does not sell, rent or assign personal data. It only discloses it when necessary for the purposes of this Policy, to the following categories of recipients:

  1. Technology providers acting as MASIVA's processors: cloud infrastructure, corporate email, customer relationship management, process automation, storage and support.
  2. Communication channel providers: Meta Platforms, Inc. for WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger, and the telecommunications carriers and their authorized channels for SMS.
  3. Analytics and advertising providers: Google, Meta and TikTok, regarding data collected through the technologies in section 14.
  4. Artificial intelligence providers: when conversational assistants are used, such as OpenAI, under section 15.
  5. External advisors: accountants, lawyers and auditors, subject to confidentiality duties.
  6. Financial institutions and payment gateways: to receive and make payments.
  7. Authorities: when there is a legal obligation or a request from a competent authority.

MASIVA selects providers with recognized industry security standards and requires, to the extent their terms of service allow, confidentiality and data protection commitments consistent with this Policy. The technical architecture of the platforms and the identity and commercial terms of the infrastructure and connectivity providers are MASIVA's trade secrets.

12. International transfers

Several of the providers listed in section 11 store or process information on servers located outside Ecuador, mainly in the United States of America. Therefore, using MASIVA's services and websites involves international transfers of personal data.

MASIVA processes that data in accordance with Ecuadorian law, prefers providers that offer contractual data protection commitments and enterprise-grade security measures, and aligns its transfers with the mechanisms, safeguards and records established by the rules issued by the SPDP. Data subjects may request information about transfers affecting their data at datospersonales@masiva.ec.

13. Source of data not obtained from the data subject

When MASIVA receives a person's data through a third party, for example when a client designates an employee as a technical contact, or when a social media ad delivers to MASIVA the data the data subject entered in the platform's form, MASIVA informs the data subject about the processing in its first communication with them and, in any case, within thirty (30) days after obtaining the data.

The analytics and advertising technologies are activated when the websites load, and MASIVA uses them only to measure the use of its websites and promote its own services, without selling or assigning the information obtained. Visitors may object to them at any time and free of charge by any of these means:

  1. Through the website's cookie notice or settings, when available.
  2. By blocking or deleting cookies in their browser settings, or by browsing in private mode.
  3. Through the opt-out tools offered by Google for Google Analytics and the ad preference options of their Meta and TikTok accounts.
  4. By writing to datospersonales@masiva.ec.

Objecting does not prevent browsing the websites. Technical or necessary technologies cannot be disabled from the website because without them it does not work properly.

14.3. Third-party information

Google, Meta and TikTok process the information they receive from these technologies under their own privacy policies, which visitors can consult on their websites. Visitors can also block or delete cookies in their browser settings and adjust their advertising preferences in their account on each social network.

15. Artificial intelligence and automated decisions

MASIVA offers its clients conversational assistants that may use language models from third-party providers. When MASIVA uses this type of assistant in its own customer service channels, it will say so at the start of the conversation and offer the option of being served by a person.

MASIVA does not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects on data subjects or similarly affect them. MASIVA's advertising campaigns may be targeted to segments defined on the Meta, Google or TikTok platforms based on interests or previous visits, which does not involve any decision on the data subject's rights and can be disabled by rejecting the technologies in section 14.

MASIVA does not use its clients' contact lists or the content of their conversations to train its own or third-party artificial intelligence models.

16. Consequences of providing or not providing data

The data requested in contracts, quotes and forms is what is necessary for the purpose indicated in each case. If the data subject does not provide it, MASIVA will not be able to handle their request, provide the service, hire or pay. Providing data to receive commercial communications is voluntary, and declining does not affect any service.

The data subject is responsible for ensuring that the data they provide is true, accurate and up to date. Incorrect data may prevent contact, billing or proper provision of the service, with no liability for MASIVA. Anyone who provides another person's data, for example when designating a contact, declares that they have that person's authorization.

18. Security measures

MASIVA applies reasonable technical and organizational measures proportionate to the risk, supported by infrastructure from internationally recognized providers. As of the date of this Policy, they include:

  1. Encryption of communications: HTTPS connections with valid SSL/TLS certificates issued by internationally recognized certificate authorities, with support for the TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3 protocols.
  2. Infrastructure: enterprise-grade cloud computing services that apply, according to their provider's specifications, encryption of data at rest and secure management of cryptographic keys.
  3. Access control: authorized users with permissions differentiated by profile, formal management of user creation, changes and removal, and strengthened authentication using one-time codes.
  4. Logging and traceability: logs of sends, conversations, administrative user activity and service events.
  5. Official channels: exclusive use of the official WhatsApp Business Platform API channels and carriers' authorized channels for SMS traffic.
  6. Backup and redundancy: automated mechanisms provided by the infrastructure provider.
  7. Organizational measures: confidentiality duties for staff and collaborators, access on a need-to-know basis, separation of each client's accounts and data, handling of campaign lists only by assigned staff and periodic review of users and access.
  8. Development and testing: use of fictitious or anonymized data in development and testing environments whenever the purpose allows.

MASIVA updates these measures as technology evolves, without reducing the level of protection achieved. It does not claim security certifications it does not hold. No system is invulnerable, so MASIVA also maintains the incident management procedure in section 19.

19. Security breaches

MASIVA maintains an internal procedure to detect, record, contain, assess and document security breaches. When a breach affects data for which MASIVA is the controller, it will notify the SPDP and any other entities required by law, within the legal deadlines, and will inform the affected data subjects when there is a risk to their rights, in clear language and with recommendations to protect themselves.

When the breach affects data that MASIVA processes as a processor, it will notify the responsible client within the legal deadline and with the information available, so that the client can meet its own notification duties.

20. Data subjects' rights

Every data subject whose data MASIVA controls may exercise the following rights free of charge:

  1. Information: to know how their data is processed, which is fulfilled through this Policy and, where applicable, through notices in collection forms and channels.
  2. Access: to know whether MASIVA processes their data and obtain a copy of it.
  3. Rectification and updating: to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  4. Deletion: to request erasure of their data when it is no longer needed, has been processed without a legal basis or consent has been withdrawn.
  5. Objection: to refuse a processing activity, in particular commercial communications.
  6. Portability: to receive their data in a structured, commonly used format, or request that it be transmitted to another controller, when technically possible.
  7. Suspension of processing: to request that processing be suspended while the accuracy of the data or the validity of an objection is verified, among other cases provided by law.
  8. Not to be subject to automated decisions with legal or significant effects.
  9. Withdraw consent at any time, without retroactive effect.
  10. File complaints with MASIVA and with the SPDP.

Some rights have limits set by law. In particular, MASIVA may keep data it must retain due to a legal obligation, a request from an authority or the defense of claims, and will explain this to the data subject in its response.

21. How to exercise your rights

21.1. Submitting the request

The data subject, or their representative, submits the request by email to datospersonales@masiva.ec, preferably using the Rights Request Form available on MASIVA's websites. It may also be submitted in writing at MASIVA's address indicated in section 2. The request must include:

  1. The data subject's first and last names and an email or address for the response.
  2. A copy of their identity document or a valid electronic signature on the request.
  3. If a representative is acting, the document proving their authority.
  4. The right being exercised and a clear description of what is requested, with supporting documents where applicable.

21.2. Procedure and deadlines

  1. Identity verification: MASIVA confirms the requester's identity to prevent a third party from accessing someone else's data.
  2. Incomplete requests: if information is missing, MASIVA will request it once within five (5) business days of receipt. The data subject will have ten (10) business days to complete it. If they do not, the request will be closed, without prejudice to submitting it again.
  3. Response: MASIVA responds within fifteen (15) days of receiving the complete request. If the request is granted, it is carried out within that same period; if it is denied, in whole or in part, MASIVA explains its reasons.
  4. Free of charge: exercising rights has no cost.
  5. Record: MASIVA keeps a record of each request and its response to demonstrate compliance.

21.3. Complaint to the authority

If the data subject receives no response within the deadline or disagrees with it, they may go to the Superintendency of Personal Data Protection, attaching proof of the request made to MASIVA.

21.4. Requests about data processed as processor

If the request concerns data that MASIVA processes on behalf of a client, section 9 applies.

22. High-volume processing

Due to the nature of its services, MASIVA continuously processes a high volume of contact data on behalf of its clients. For that processing, MASIVA undertakes to keep a record of processing activities, to assess risks and, where applicable, the impact on data subjects' rights, to periodically review its security measures and to assist its clients with their own assessments, in accordance with the rules issued by the SPDP.

23. Staff, confidentiality and training

All staff and collaborators who access personal data are bound by confidentiality duties that continue after their relationship with MASIVA ends. Access is assigned according to each person's duties and revoked when those duties end. MASIVA communicates this Policy to its staff and trains them in the secure handling of information, especially clients' campaign lists. Non-compliance is handled under internal rules and labor and civil law.

24. Relationship with contracts

This Policy is general in nature. The specific terms of the processing MASIVA performs on behalf of each client are set out in the service contract, the Data Processing Agreement and, where applicable, the Confidentiality and Personal Data Processing Agreement for Messaging Campaigns. In the relationship with each client, those instruments prevail over this Policy on the matters they expressly regulate.

25. Changes to this policy

MASIVA reviews this Policy at least once a year and whenever the law, its services or its infrastructure change. The current version is published on its websites with its version number and date. When changes are substantial, MASIVA will also communicate them to its clients by email and, where applicable, request data subjects' consent again. Previous versions are kept as a historical record.

26. Governing law and supervisory authority

This Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Ecuador. The supervisory authority for personal data protection is the Superintendency of Personal Data Protection.

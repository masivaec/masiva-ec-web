1. Purpose

This Policy sets out the rules that clients of MASIVA-EC S.A. (hereinafter, MASIVA) and their users must follow when using MASIVA's communication platforms and services. Its purpose is to protect the people who receive messages, preserve the reputation and continuity of the channels shared by all clients, and ensure compliance with personal data protection, telecommunications and consumer protection laws.

MASIVA's platforms are tools. The decision of whom to write to, what to say and when belongs exclusively to the client, which is the controller of its recipients' data and of its communications.

2. Scope and acceptance

This Policy applies to all of MASIVA's services, including text messaging (SMS), the WhatsApp Business Platform API and other instant messaging and social media channels, bulk and transactional email, email sending infrastructure (SMTP), conversational assistants and omnichannel platforms, under whatever platform, subdomain or brand name they are provided, and to campaigns that MASIVA runs on the client's instructions.

The client accepts this Policy by contracting any service, signing a quote or service order or using the platforms. The client is responsible for compliance with this Policy by all users to whom it gives access to its account, by its employees and by the third parties on whose behalf it acts.

3. Relationship with contracts and channel policies

This Policy forms an integral part of the service contracts, data processing agreements and confidentiality agreements that MASIVA signs with its clients, and complements them. In case of conflict, whatever the contract expressly regulates prevails.

In addition to this Policy, the client must comply with the policies, terms and requirements of each channel provider, which may be stricter and change without notice, in particular the policies of Meta Platforms, Inc. for the WhatsApp Business Platform API, Instagram and Messenger, those of the telecommunications carriers for the SMS channel and those of mailbox providers for the email channel.

4. Lawful basis for recipients

The client declares, warrants and undertakes to:

  1. Have, for each recipient, the legal basis required by Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection to process their data and write to them through the chosen channel.
  2. Have each recipient's prior and express authorization to send them messages for commercial, advertising, promotional or proselytizing purposes, as required by telecommunications, data protection and consumer protection laws and by the channel's policies.
  3. Use only databases collected lawfully by the client itself or by third parties entitled to share them. The use of purchased, rented or unlawfully assigned databases, or databases extracted from public sources, social networks, directories or through automated harvesting techniques, is prohibited.
  4. Keep evidence of recipients' authorizations for the term of the service and the legal period thereafter, and deliver it to MASIVA within forty-eight (48) hours of a request, when required by a channel provider, carrier or authority.
  5. Inform recipients about the processing of their data through its own privacy policy, which must state that it uses technology providers for its communications.
  6. When acting on behalf of a third party, such as an agency for its client, have sufficient mandate and authorization from that third party, and be accountable to MASIVA as if it were the controller.

5. Rules by channel

5.1. Text messaging (SMS)

  1. Use each route only for the authorized type of message. The informational or transactional route is for notifications, verification codes, confirmations, alerts, reminders and communications tied to an existing relationship, and may not be used for commercial content. Commercial messages are sent only through the commercial route enabled for the client and, when the channels require it, only to numbers previously registered in the lists they manage.
  2. Clearly identify the client as the sender within the text of each message.
  3. Use only texts and sender IDs approved by the channels, and respect the sending hours they allow.
  4. Include a free and simple opt-out mechanism in commercial messages.

5.2. WhatsApp Business Platform API and other Meta channels

  1. Start conversations only with people who have agreed to receive WhatsApp messages from the client, in accordance with Meta's policies, and only with Meta-approved templates when the conversation was not started by the user.
  2. Respect the category of each template, without using utility or authentication templates for marketing content.
  3. Honor opt-out requests received in the conversation immediately.
  4. Acknowledge that Meta assigns each number a quality rating and messaging limits that depend on users' reactions, which it defines and changes unilaterally, and that it may restrict or block the client's number or account for violations, with no liability for MASIVA.

5.3. Email

  1. Send only to addresses that have given their authorization or that have a current relationship with the client that justifies the communication.
  2. Identify the sender with a real name and address of the client, without misleading subject lines.
  3. Include in every commercial email a visible, working unsubscribe link that is processed automatically.
  4. When sending from its own domain, keep that domain's authentication records correctly configured as MASIVA indicates.
  5. Maintain list quality: the hard bounce rate of each send must not exceed five percent (5%) and the spam complaint rate must not exceed three per thousand (0.3%).

5.4. Conversational assistants and artificial intelligence

  1. Inform users that they are interacting with an automated assistant and offer the option of being served by a person when the nature of the inquiry requires it.
  2. Approve and keep up to date the information the assistant provides, especially rates, prices, terms, requirements and any data that creates expectations for the user. The client is solely responsible for that information.
  3. Not use the assistant to make decisions with legal or significant effects on people, such as approving or denying loans, services or benefits, without human involvement and without meeting the requirements of applicable law.
  4. Not request sensitive data, passwords, PINs or full card details through the assistant, except through the secure mechanisms required by the client's sector regulations.

6. Opt-outs and suppression list

The client must honor every opt-out request within forty-eight (48) hours of receipt and remove the recipient from its lists on all relevant channels. MASIVA may apply to the client's sends a Suppression List with destinations that have opted out, filed complaints or are invalid. Applying that list does not constitute a breach by MASIVA and gives no right to a credit for messages not sent for that reason.

7. Prohibited content

Sending messages through any channel that do any of the following is prohibited:

  1. Impersonate people, companies, financial institutions, public entities or third-party brands, or include links intended to improperly capture credentials, passwords, banking details or personal information.
  2. Are misleading or create confusion about the sender, the content, the price or the terms of an offer.
  3. Promote illegal activities, fraud, pyramid schemes, unauthorized betting or gambling, or the sale of products or services whose sale is prohibited or restricted without the corresponding authorization.
  4. Contain sexual or obscene material or involve children or adolescents in any way in inappropriate contexts.
  5. Incite violence, hatred or discrimination, or are defamatory, threatening or harassing.
  6. Infringe the intellectual property, image or privacy rights of third parties.
  7. Contain malicious software, links to compromised websites or any code that could harm devices or systems.
  8. Disclose sensitive data or data of children and adolescents without the enhanced legal basis required by law.
  9. Violate electoral or advertising rules or any other provision in force, or the policies of the channel used.

8. Prohibited practices

  1. Sending unsolicited messages or sending to the same recipient with disproportionate frequency.
  2. Sending to recipients who have opted out or who are on the Suppression List.
  3. Rotating sender IDs, numbers, domains or accounts, altering approved texts or using any technique intended to evade the channels' filters, controls or limits.
  4. Using link shorteners or redirects that hide the real destination for misleading purposes.
  5. Testing databases to identify valid numbers or addresses.
  6. Reselling, sublicensing or giving third parties access to the platforms or contracted credits without MASIVA's written authorization.
  7. Sharing credentials with unauthorized people, attempting to access other clients' accounts or data, deliberately overloading the platform or breaching its security measures.
  8. Reverse engineering, extracting information from the platform by unauthorized automated means or using it to develop a competing service.

9. Regulated sectors and debt collection

When the client is a financial institution, a popular and solidarity economy organization, an insurer, a portfolio manager or a third party acting on behalf of any of them, it warrants that its communications, in particular those related to collections, portfolio recovery and payment demands, comply with its sector's rules, including those on treatment of debtors, financial consumer protection, permitted hours and the prohibition on contacting third parties unrelated to the debt. MASIVA only provides the sending infrastructure and does not review or approve the content, frequency, timing or recipients of those communications.

10. Accounts, credentials and users

The client is responsible for safeguarding its account's access credentials and integration keys, assigning permissions to its users and revoking access for those who no longer need it. It must notify MASIVA immediately of any unauthorized use. Sends and usage carried out with its credentials until notification and blocking are charged to its account.

11. Monitoring by MASIVA

MASIVA has no obligation to review the content or recipients of the client's messages. Nevertheless, to protect the channels and comply with the law, MASIVA may automatically monitor each account's volumes, delivery, bounce, complaint, opt-out and block rates and, when there are signs of non-compliance or when required by a channel provider or authority, review the sends and the information strictly necessary to verify compliance with this Policy. This monitoring is carried out confidentially and in accordance with the data processing agreement signed with the client.

12. Measures in case of non-compliance

Depending on the severity, recurrence and risk to recipients or channels, MASIVA may take one or more of the following measures, which do not constitute a breach on its part:

MeasureWhen it applies
Warning and deadline to correctMinor violations or first signs, such as bounce or complaint rates above the thresholds in section 5.3
Pausing campaigns or limiting volumeRepeatedly exceeding thresholds, degradation of the number's or sender's quality, complaints from recipients or a channel provider's request
Requirement to clean lists or provide evidenceDoubts about the origin of a list or about recipients' authorizations
Immediate suspension without prior noticeSending prohibited content under section 7, impersonation, phishing, bulk unsolicited sending, use of purchased lists, risk to platform security or an authority's request. MASIVA will notify the suspension and its reason within twenty-four (24) hours
Termination of the serviceSerious or repeated violations, refusal to correct, or closure of the channel by the provider for reasons attributable to the client

Measures taken for reasons attributable to the client give no right to a refund, credit reinstatement, compensation or damages, and do not suspend invoicing of the agreed amounts. MASIVA may keep evidence of the violation and share it with channel providers and competent authorities when appropriate.

13. Liability and indemnification

The client is solely responsible for the content of its messages, the lawfulness of its databases and its sending decisions. It shall hold harmless MASIVA, its shareholders, directors, staff and collaborators against any claim, penalty, fine or action, including reasonable and documented defense costs, arising from a breach of this Policy. Fines, penalties or charges that channel providers or authorities impose on MASIVA because of the client's sends will be passed on to the client in full, with the supporting documents issued by whoever imposes them, and may be deducted from the available balance.

14. Reporting abuse

Anyone who receives unwanted or fraudulent messages sent through MASIVA's platforms can report it to datospersonales@masiva.ec, stating the number or address that received the message, the date and, if possible, a screenshot. MASIVA will review the report, take the measures in this Policy when appropriate and forward the request to the sending company in accordance with its Privacy and Personal Data Protection Policy.

15. Changes

MASIVA may update this Policy to reflect changes in the law, in the channels' policies or in its services. The current version is published on its websites, and substantial changes are communicated to clients by email at least fifteen (15) days in advance, unless they result from a legal requirement, an authority or a channel provider, in which case they apply from publication. Continued use of the services implies acceptance of the current version.

16. Governing law

This Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Ecuador. Disputes will be resolved through the dispute resolution mechanism agreed in the service contract and, in its absence, before the competent courts of the Metropolitan District of Quito.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the WhatsApp Business app and the official API?
The free app is designed for a small business working from one phone. The official API is the enterprise version: several agents on the same number from one platform, campaigns with Meta-approved templates, chatbots and connection to your systems. It does not depend on a phone that stays on.
Can my number be banned for sending campaigns?
Not with the official API. Campaigns are sent with templates reviewed by Meta, through the channel Meta designed for businesses. Bans happen when unofficial tools are used to automate the regular app.
How much does it cost to send WhatsApp messages?
Meta charges for each outgoing message according to its current rate card by country and category (marketing, utility, authentication or service), and your company pays it directly from its business portfolio. Our proposal details the implementation, the subscription and those fees.
Can I keep using the number on my phone?
No. A number active on the official API is handled from the platform (web and mobile app), with your whole team. If you use that number in the app today, we migrate it; if you prefer, we activate a new one.
What do I need to get started?
A phone number that is not active in the WhatsApp app, a Meta business portfolio in your company's name (we help you create or verify it) and the text of the messages you want to send. If your business portfolio is already verified, activation takes minutes; if not, one week at most.
Who writes the templates?
Your team, with our guidance on categories and best practices so Meta approves them the first time. We build the automation flows and the chatbot.

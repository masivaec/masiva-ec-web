The platform

Marketing or transactional: you decide, the platform does both

One account for this month's campaign and for the automated emails your system triggers every day.

Drag-and-drop editor

Design professional emails in minutes, with no coding. Responsive templates that look great on mobile.

Careful deliverability

Your own sending domain, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, a reputable IP and SSL. We set it all up for you.

Audiences and variables

Upload contacts from CSV or TXT, segment by list and personalize each email with your own fields.

Automatic bounces and unsubscribes

Real-time reports

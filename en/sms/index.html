Options

Three ways to send SMS, depending on how your company works

From our platform, from your own system via API, or both at once. You choose; the account and the packages are the same.

Web platform

Upload your contacts, write, schedule and measure from your browser. Nothing to install; ideal for campaigns and reminders run by your sales or collections team.

REST API from your system

Your core system, CRM, ERP or app sends the SMS directly: passcodes, confirmations, transaction alerts. Clear documentation and ready-made examples for your development team.

Both at once

Your system triggers the automated messages (passcodes, notices) via API, and your team launches campaigns from the platform. One account, one package, one report.

    Common questions

    Frequently asked questions about SMS

    Do you send SMS to every carrier in Ecuador?
    Yes. SMS reach Claro, Tigo (formerly Movistar), CNT and Tuenti lines through a local short code, with your company as the sender and a delivery report for every message.
    Does MASIVA send SMS outside Ecuador?
    Yes. From the same platform you can send SMS to more than 200 countries, with rates per destination published on this page and no prior text approval.
    How much does it cost to send an SMS to another country?
    It depends on the destination. The international coverage table on this page shows the price per country; for example, search for Colombia, Peru, the United States or Spain to see its rate.
    Can I send SMS from my own system?
    Yes. Besides the web platform, you have a documented REST API to connect your core system, CRM or ERP and send SMS within Ecuador or abroad from your own processes.
    What is the difference between transactional and advertising SMS?
    Transactional SMS carries information the customer must receive (passcodes, alerts, reminders, confirmations). Advertising SMS promotes or builds loyalty with non-urgent messages, and has content and timing rules that we help you follow.

Options

Two ways to build it, depending on what you need

We recommend the one that fits your case, with its advantages and limits explained without jargon.

Hand-coded website

For presentation sites that rarely change: companies, credit unions, institutions and campaign landing pages.

  • Loads in under two seconds, even on a phone using mobile data
  • No plugins to update and none of WordPress's typical vulnerabilities
  • Complete freedom in design and animation
  • Near-zero maintenance cost

WordPress website

For when your team publishes content often: an active blog, news, a changing catalog or an online store.

  • Edit text and images without depending on anyone
  • Blog, store and advanced forms ready to go
  • Custom design with Elementor Pro or Divi
  • Maintenance plan and backups
AI-powered updates

Want a hand-coded site but worried about how to update it?

We deliver it connected to our AI update system. Write or send a voice note with what you want to change, and your site updates and publishes itself within minutes. Updates in the palm of your hand: the AI does the work while you relax.
